ELECTRICAL

Our typical design includes electrical outlets layout, equipment electrical supply, lighting design, phone outlets layout, circuiting, electrical riser diagram, grounding diagram, electrical load calculations, panels schedule. We also specialize in reliable power design for sensitive applications requiring lighting protection, special grounding systems, generator backup power, and uninterruptible power supplies. We also specialize in high voltage distribution system design, energy efficiency solutions, roadway lighting, and site lighting.

PLUMBING

We have designed many car wash facilities, dentist offices with vacuum and compressed air, restaurants with grease waste plumbing and grease trap sizing septic tank systems; we also specialize in building roof drainage design.
